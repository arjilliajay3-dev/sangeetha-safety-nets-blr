### 3. Koramangala (South-Central Corridor - PIN: 560034 / 560095)
- **Housing Profile:** Premium residential villas, independent luxury homes, builder floors, and upscale mid-rise apartment societies.
- **Core Hazards:** Open sit-out balconies vulnerable to pigeons, monkey movement along tree lines, aesthetic demands requiring transparent invisible grills.
- **Primary Keywords:**

### 4. Electronic City (South IT Hub - PIN: 560100)
- **Housing Profile:** Massive residential townships with 1,000+ units, tech corridor gated communities (Phase 1 & Phase 2).
